PhotoShare Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

PhotoShare is designed to allow people to share photos directly with nearby devices while minimizing the collection of personal information.

Information We Collect

PhotoShare does not require a user account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, passwords, or other personal account information.

Photos

Photos selected or shared through PhotoShare are transferred directly between participating nearby devices. PhotoShare does not upload or store shared photos on a PhotoShare server or cloud service.

Nearby Connections

PhotoShare uses device features such as local networking and Bluetooth to discover and communicate with nearby devices. These features are used only to provide nearby photo-sharing functionality.

Camera and Photo Library

PhotoShare may request access to the camera to scan session QR codes and permission to save photos to the user's Photos library. These permissions are used only for features requested by the user.

Data Sharing

PhotoShare does not sell personal information or shared photos to third parties.

Data Retention

PhotoShare does not maintain a server-side database of user accounts or shared photos.

Children's Privacy

PhotoShare does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as PhotoShare's features change. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

Contact information for privacy or support questions will be provided through PhotoShare's official support page.
